Web app and optional account

Pro subscribers can also use the web app at nclexrapidnursingreview.com/app after signing in with Apple in the iPhone app (Settings → Sync and web access). The web app is part of your Pro subscription and doesn't sell anything; it works while your subscription is active. The account is personal: please don't share it.
