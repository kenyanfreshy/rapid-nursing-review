# Privacy Policy — Rapid Nursing Review

*Last updated: October 12, 2026*

Rapid Nursing Review ("the app") is a study app for nursing students, based on the book *Rapid Nursing Review: All Body
Systems at a Glance* by Alex K. Korir, MSN, RN, CCRN-CMC. This policy explains what the app does with information.

## What stays on your device

Your study data stays on your phone or computer: your answers and scores, study sessions, bookmarks, study plan, notes on
questions you report, and the name you enter. The app has no accounts and does not send this data to us or anyone else.
You can export it yourself from Settings, and you can delete it at any time with "Reset data" or by removing the app.

## Purchases

If you buy Rapid Nursing Review Pro, the purchase is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your
payment details. To check whether you have Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an
anonymous app user ID, your purchase history for this app and basic device information (such as the app version and the
store country), and uses them only to provide and restore your purchases. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at
https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

## What we don't do

- No advertising, and no advertising or tracking SDKs.
- No analytics that identify you.
- We don't sell or share your information.
- We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or health data.

## Links to other websites

Some explanations link to outside sources. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

## Children

The app is for adult nursing students and is not directed at children under 13.

## Educational use

The app is for education only. It is not medical advice and does not predict exam results.
