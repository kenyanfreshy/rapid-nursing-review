Sign-in is optional. The app works fully without an account, offline, exactly as in 1.0; no feature or purchase requires signing in.
• Where: Settings (tap the initials at the top right) → first card, "Sync and web access" → Sign in with Apple.

• Sign in with Apple is the only sign-in method.

ACCOUNT DELETION (Guideline 5.1.1(v)): Settings → Sync and web access → Delete account → type DELETE → Delete account. This permanently deletes the account and its synced data on our server and, when deleted in the iPhone app, revokes the Sign in with Apple token. Deletion is also available in the web app.

NO PURCHASES ON THE WEB: Pro is sold only in the iPhone app through In-App Purchase. The web app shows no prices, sells nothing and has no links to outside payment. A web visitor without an active subscription is told to subscribe in the iPhone app.

TO TEST THE WEB VERSION (optional): buy any Pro plan with the sandbox account in the app, tap Sign in with Apple in Settings, then open https://nclexrapidnursingreview.com/app/ in Safari on a Mac and sign in with the same Apple ID. Progress made on either device appears on the other within a few seconds, or tap Sync now.

SCREENSHOTS: To protect the author's book, screenshots and screen recording are blocked only while a book page is open. They work everywhere else in the app.

FREE CONTENT: About 400 questions, one NGN case study, tutor mode and the book's preview pages are free.
