Prepare for the NCLEX-RN with the study app built on Rapid Nursing Review: All Body Systems at a Glance by Alex K. Korir, MSN, RN, CCRN-CMC, a critical care nurse and nursing author.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 3,200 NCLEX-style questions across all 19 NCLEX topic areas, organized by the NCSBN Client Needs categories
• 982 Next Generation NCLEX (NGN) format items: select-all-that-apply, matrix and ordered response
• 30 unfolding NGN case studies with a full client chart (nurses' notes, vitals, labs and orders) and six clinical-judgment steps each, including highlight, cloze, matrix and bow-tie items scored with NGN partial-credit rules
• A rationale for every answer option, a high-yield takeaway, and sources on every question: the book page it comes from or APA references to nursing textbooks and clinical guidelines
• 1,295 questions linked to the exact page of the book, so you can open the page that explains the answer
• A 75-question blueprint exam weighted to the 2026 NCLEX-RN test plan, with results by client need
• Tutor mode that explains as you go, and a timed exam mode that feels like test day
• The complete book inside the app, page by page, with search and bookmarks

STUDY ON YOUR SCHEDULE
• A personal study plan built around your exam date: choose one or more courses (Pediatrics, Maternal & Newborn, Pharmacology, Cardiovascular and more) and get a daily target
• Study reminders at the time and days you choose, and a repeating event you can add to your own calendar
• Progress tracking by topic and client need, a daily streak, missed-question review and bookmarks
• Share your progress by email or social media
• Works offline: every question and every page of the book is stored on your phone
