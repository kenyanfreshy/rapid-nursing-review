## Common questions

**Restore Pro on a new phone:** open the app → tap **Upgrade** (or Settings → See Pro plans) → **Restore Purchases**,
signed in with the same Apple ID (iPhone/iPad) or Google account (Android) you bought with.

**Cancel a subscription:** on your iPhone or iPad, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Rapid Nursing Review →
Cancel Subscription. On Android, open the Google Play app → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions →
Rapid Nursing Review → Cancel subscription.

**Refunds:** requested through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, or through Google Play (play.google.com → your
order history), under that store's policies.

**Study on a Mac or any computer (Pro):** in the iPhone app, open **Settings → Sync and web access** and tap
**Sign in with Apple**. Then go to **nclexrapidnursingreview.com/app** on your computer and sign in with the same Apple ID.
Your progress syncs both ways. The web app is for existing Pro subscribers; subscribe in the iPhone app. Sign in and the
web app are on iPhone and iPad for now; on Android, progress stays on your phone.

**"We couldn't find an active subscription" on the web:** on your iPhone, tap Restore Purchases, then sign in with Apple in
Settings → Sync and web access using the same Apple ID as on the web, and tap Check again.

**Move or back up your progress:** sign in with Apple (optional) to keep it in sync, or use Settings → Export to save a copy.
Without an account, progress stays on the device where you study.

**Delete your account:** Settings → Sync and web access → Delete account (in the app or on the web). This deletes the
account and the progress synced with it; it doesn't cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription.

**Delete the data on this device:** Settings → Reset data, or delete the app.

**Report a question:** inside any question, tap **Report**. Tell us what looks wrong and we'll review it.
