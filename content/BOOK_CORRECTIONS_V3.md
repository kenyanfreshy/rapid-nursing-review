# Book points to check (found while reviewing the v3 questions)

The second reviewers found these places where the book is outdated or imprecise. The questions were fixed so they don't
depend on these points, but you may want to update the book in its next edition.

| Page | Topic | What the book says | Current practice |
|---|---|---|---|
| 74, 76 | Peptic ulcer disease | Lists IgG antibody (serology) as an H. pylori test | Serology can't tell active from past infection and guidelines advise against it; use urea breath or stool antigen tests |
| 118 | Acute kidney injury | High-carbohydrate, low-protein diet | KDIGO advises against restricting protein in AKI, and protein is increased for clients on renal replacement therapy |
| 143 | Parkinson's disease | Haloperidol and metoclopramide described as dopamine-depleting | Both are dopamine antagonists (they block dopamine receptors) |
| 146 | ALS | Cognition is unaffected | Most people keep clear thinking, but some develop cognitive or behavioral change (ALS-FTD spectrum) |
| 164 | DIC | Activated protein C listed as a treatment | Drotrecogin alfa (activated protein C) was withdrawn from the market in 2011 |

Full per-item notes are in `content/verification/*-v3.json` and `content/verification/cases-a.json` / `cases-b.json`.
