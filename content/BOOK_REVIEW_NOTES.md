- **p. 58:** Pages 58 and 59 are duplicates: both repeat the same 'Acute Venous and Artery Occlusion' comparison table (definition, cause, risk factors, pathophysiology, symptoms) with only a slight layout offset.
- **p. 58:** 'Positive Homan's sign' is listed as a DVT symptom; the sign is spelled Homans' and is generally considered unreliable for diagnosing DVT. Heading says 'Artery Occlusion' while the table of contents uses 'arterial occlusion'.
