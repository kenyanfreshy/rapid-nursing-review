Delete your Rapid Nursing Review account

Rapid Nursing Review works without an account. If you signed in (Settings → Sync and web access) to sync your progress or use the web app, you can delete that account and everything stored with it at any time.

In the app or web app (fastest)

1. Open Rapid Nursing Review on your phone, or the web app at nclexrapidnursingreview.com/app. 2. Go to Settings → Sync and web access. 3. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

The account and its synced data are deleted immediately.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email alexkorirbooks@gmail.com from the address you signed in with (or tell us which Apple or Google account you used), with the subject "Delete my Rapid Nursing Review account". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your Rapid Nursing Review account: sign-in ID, and your email address and name if you shared them.
  • The progress synced with it: answers, missed questions, bookmarks, notes, study plan and exam scores.

Nothing is kept after deletion.

What stays

  • Progress stored on your own phone stays there until you delete the app or choose Settings → Reset data.
  • A Pro subscription is billed by the App Store or Google Play, not by us. Deleting your account does not cancel it. Cancel it in your Apple Account settings, or in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Purchase records kept by Apple, Google and our subscription service RevenueCat follow their own retention rules. To have RevenueCat's record of your account ID removed, ask us by email.
