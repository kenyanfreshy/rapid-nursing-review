Using the web app

The web app is for Rapid Nursing Review Pro subscribers. Subscribe in the iPhone app, then open Settings → Sync and web access and tap Sign in with Apple. On your computer, go to nclexrapidnursingreview.com/app and sign in with the same Apple ID. Your progress follows you.
